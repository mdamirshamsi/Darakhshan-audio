Help Center

Welcome to BSEB CLASS 10TH URDU LEARNING.

Our Help Center is here to help you use the app, access educational content, practice questions, track your learning progress, and resolve common issues.

Getting Started

1. Download and open the app.
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. You may sign in using your Google or Facebook account.
4. Select the required learning content.
5. Study lessons and practice questions.
6. Use quizzes and other available features to improve your preparation.

Account & Login

If you are unable to log in:

* Check your internet connection.
* Make sure you are using the correct login method.
* If you use Google Sign-In, make sure you are signed in to the correct Google account.
* Close and reopen the app.
* Update the app to the latest available version.

Learning Content

You can use the app to access Class 10th Urdu learning materials, lessons, questions, answers, quizzes, and other educational resources available within the app.

Learning Progress

Where supported, your learning progress, quiz results, and related information may be saved to your account so that you can continue your learning.

Payments

If the app offers paid content, subscriptions, or other purchases:

* Make sure your payment information is entered correctly.
* Check your internet connection.
* Do not share your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, password, or other confidential payment information with anyone.

Advertisements

The app may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.
